# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, professional, and evidence-driven engineering community.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate with technical clarity and respect.
- Focus on reproducible engineering work.
- Provide constructive review feedback.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation
- Misrepresentation of test results or capabilities
- Sharing confidential or sensitive information without authorization

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for violations.
