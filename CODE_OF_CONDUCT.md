# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, professional, and technically rigorous collaboration environment.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Prioritize technical clarity over personal opinion.
- Accept and give feedback in good faith.
- Keep safety, ethics, and accuracy central in UAV/UAS discussions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hostility
- Deliberate misinformation
- Sharing sensitive or private data without permission
- Unsafe guidance presented as production-ready practice

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for behavior that violates this code.
