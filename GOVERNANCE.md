# Governance

## Purpose

Define how technical decisions are made and validated across the HDCS engineering portfolio.

## Decision Model

Major technical decisions should include:

1. Engineering reason
2. Assumptions
3. Risks
4. Alternatives considered
5. Measurable success criteria

## Repository Maturity Policy

Allowed status labels:

- concept
- prototype
- ground-tested
- flight-tested
- production-candidate

Status labels must reflect available evidence, not intent.

## Technical Review Requirements

Before major merges, assess:

- architecture coherence
- interface clarity
- safety implications
- cybersecurity boundaries
- reproducibility and maintainability

## Claims Policy

The project must not:

- invent test results
- claim certifications that do not exist
- claim operational capability without evidence
